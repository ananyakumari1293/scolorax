# 📚 ABOUT THE PROJECT

ScoloraX is a modern scholarship discovery platform built to simplify how students explore educational opportunities.

Students often spend hours browsing scattered websites, missing deadlines and struggling to find scholarships relevant to their degree or career path.

ScoloraX brings everything together into one clean and student-friendly experience.

The platform focuses on:

* curated scholarship opportunities
* degree-specific filtering
* clean and accessible UI
* simplified scholarship discovery
* organized academic resources

---

# 🚀 WHAT SCOLORAX OFFERS

### 🎯 Curated Opportunities

Discover scholarships collected and organized in one place.

### 🎓 Degree-Specific Discovery

Browse opportunities tailored for:

* B.Tech
* MBA
* MCA
* BCA
* BBA
* BSc
  and more.

### 🔖 Save & Revisit

Bookmark scholarships and revisit important opportunities anytime.

Built with a responsive and minimal interface focused on clarity and accessibility.
